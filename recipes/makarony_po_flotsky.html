Makarony po Flotsky

image source: curiouscuisiniere.com

Description

This recipe is quick and easy to make. It requires few ingredients on hand to make a delicious and nutritious meal. It became a staple with the Soviet navy.

Ingredients

  • minced meat
  • pasta
  • an onion
  • baking oil

Steps

  1. Cut up the onion.
  2. Add onion chunks and seasoning to the ground beef.
  3. Bake the ground beef while you boil the pasta.
  4. Combine everything together.
