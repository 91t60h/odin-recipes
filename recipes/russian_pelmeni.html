Russian Pelmeni

image source: allrecipes.com

Description

This recipe takes about 1 hour of preparation time followed by another hour of actual sculpting of the dumplings. It takes about 20 minutes to boil them once they are sculpted for a total of 2 hours and 20 minutes. This recipe accounts for about 100 pelmeni. One serving is usually about 10 to 20 pelmeni. Pelmeni is life.

Ingredients

  • Dough:
    • 1 large egg
    • 1 teaspoon of vegetable oil
    • 1 teaspoon of salt
    • ¾ cup warm water
    • 3 cups flour
    • 1 tbs flour
  • Filling:
    • 18 ounce ground meat
    • 1 small onion chopped
    • 1½ tbs ice cold water
    • 1 tbs coarse salt
    • freshly ground pepper to taste

Steps

  1. Gather all the ingredients
  2. Make dough: Combine egg, oil, and salt in a liquid measuring cup; add enough warm water to fill to 1 cup
  3. Pour into a large bowl and knead in 3 cups flour until smooth and elastic.
